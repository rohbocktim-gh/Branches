Privacy Policy

Branches Summer Delight · Effective October 14, 2026

Branches Summer Delight ("the app") is a puzzle game made by an independent developer. This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

Information the app itself keeps

The app does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email or other personal details. Your puzzle progress, petals, streaks, unlocked skins and settings are stored only on your device. The developer does not receive or have access to this information. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads using Google AdMob, a service provided by Google LLC. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may automatically collect information from your device, including:

  • Device identifiers, such as your device's advertising ID
  • Approximate location, derived from your IP address
  • Information about how you interact with ads and the app
  • Diagnostic information, such as crash logs and performance data

This information is collected and processed by Google, is sent over an encrypted connection, and is used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Depending on where you live, you may be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown. You can learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choices

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device). You can also change your ad consent choice from within the app where that option is offered.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Sharing

The developer does not sell your information and does not collect personal information that could be shared. Data collected by Google AdMob is handled under Google's policies, linked above.
