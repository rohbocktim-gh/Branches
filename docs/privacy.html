Branches Summer Delight – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Branches Summer Delight ("the app") is a number puzzle game for Android. This policy explains what information the app does and does not collect.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information ourselves.

Your game progress (puzzle numbers, days played, streaks, petals and unlocked skins) is stored only on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app.

The app loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means your device contacts Google's servers to download them. Google does not receive any game data this way. See Google Fonts privacy.

Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and approximate location derived from your IP address. Where required (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice later in the app's consent options when offered.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in your Android device settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads). Learn more about how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes
